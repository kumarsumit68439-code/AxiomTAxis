Suite overview

Everything free, in one bot

Music

YouTube player

Type /play song name. No URL needed. The bot joins your voice channel and posts a mini player with pause, resume, 10s seek and stop buttons plus a live progress bar.

AI

AI chat, no mention

Pick a channel with /ai channel and the bot answers every message there. Choose from 8 providers and dozens of models, and set your own system prompt.

Community

Welcome messages

Greet new members with your own text. Placeholders: user, username, server and member count.

Utility

Custom commands

Create text commands for rules, links or FAQs with /command add, then run them with /c name.

Control

Per-server settings

Every server has its own AI channel, model, prompt, welcome setup and commands. Only members with Manage Server can change them.

FAQ

Does the bot reply in my AI channel without a mention?

Yes. After /ai channel #channel it answers every message in that channel. It ignores other bots.

Why do AI chat and welcome need extra intents?

They read message content and member joins, which Discord treats as privileged intents. If they are off, the bot still runs in basic mode with music and commands.

Is anything paid?

No. There are no plans or premium features.

Music does not start. What should I check?

Join a voice channel first and make sure the bot can Connect and Speak there. The invite link already asks for those permissions.
