FAQ

Does the bot reply in my AI channel without a mention?

Yes. After /ai channel #channel it answers every message in that channel. It ignores other bots.

Why do AI chat and welcome need extra intents?

They read message content and member joins, which Discord treats as privileged intents. If they are off, the bot still runs in basic mode with music and commands.

Is anything paid?

No. There are no plans, no premium features and no payment required. Everything is free.

Music does not start. What should I check?

Join a voice channel first and make sure the bot can Connect and Speak there. The invite link already asks for those permissions.
